Our Story

A Table for
Every Family

Ember & Oak was born from a simple belief — that the best moments in life happen around a shared meal. Founded in 2009 by the Harlow family, we've spent over fifteen years perfecting recipes passed down through generations.

Every cut of meat is sourced locally. Every sauce is made from scratch. Every guest is treated like they've come home.
